DocumentFormat.OpenXml SDK - Third-Party Notice
================================================

These assemblies are Microsoft's official Open XML SDK for .NET, used here to
generate DOCX reports directly (no Pandoc, no Microsoft Word installation, and
no admin rights required to load them).

Source:  https://www.nuget.org/packages/DocumentFormat.OpenXml
         https://www.nuget.org/packages/DocumentFormat.OpenXml.Framework
         https://www.nuget.org/packages/System.IO.Packaging
Project: https://github.com/dotnet/Open-XML-SDK
License: MIT (see https://github.com/dotnet/Open-XML-SDK/blob/main/LICENSE)
Version: 3.5.1 (DocumentFormat.OpenXml / DocumentFormat.OpenXml.Framework)
         10.0.11 (System.IO.Packaging)

Layout:
  Lib\OpenXml\Desktop\  - net46 build, used under Windows PowerShell 5.1
                          (.NET Framework). Relies on the built-in WindowsBase
                          assembly already present on every Windows machine.
  Lib\OpenXml\Core\     - net8.0 build, used under PowerShell 7+ (.NET).
                          Includes System.IO.Packaging.dll because .NET does
                          not ship the WindowsBase packaging APIs.

These DLLs are loaded in-process via Add-Type. They are not installed,
registered, or added to PSModulePath, and do not require administrative
rights or Microsoft Word to be present on the machine.
